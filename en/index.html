Questions about training with 迹时运动

Which sports work with Auto Cut?

Auto Cut supports tennis, badminton, and table tennis video.

Are AI Coach and Match Review available for every sport?

No. AI Coach and Match Review currently support table tennis. We keep this boundary explicit so every public claim matches the app.

What can I learn from Match Review?

For supported table-tennis matches, 迹时运动 brings together technical data, tactical data, ball speed, placement, shot patterns, match phases, and key moments so you can understand more than the final score.

How is a 迹时运动 AI training plan personalized?

The plan analyzes your goal, training focus, weekly frequency, session length, difficulty, available setup, the injury information you choose to provide, and every training or match activity you add to your Growth Profile.
